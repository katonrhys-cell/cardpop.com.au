Who we are

CardPop is Australia’s trusted digital card show — a modern, community-first marketplace where collectors connect, trade, explore and grow their collections with confidence.

To create Australia’s most trusted and engaging digital card show, where collectors can connect, trade, explore and grow their collections confidently.

Vision

To become the leading global ecosystem for collectible-card enthusiasts by combining community, trust, marketplace functionality and hobby culture.

Positioning

A trusted, modern, community-first marketplace inspired by collector culture, convention energy and the accessibility of today’s best digital platforms.

The mark

The CardPop mark pairs a graded-slab icon with a confident serif wordmark. The “Pop” always carries the brand red. Three approved lockups cover every background.

Clear space & minimum size

Maintain clear space equal to the height of the “C” in CardPop around all marks. Minimum size: 80px wide for digital, 25mm for print. The slab icon and wordmark may be used together, or the wordmark alone.

07 · Logo Usage

Do & don’t

The mark is the most recognisable expression of the brand. Protect it — consistency here is what makes CardPop feel established and trustworthy.

✓ Do
  • Maintain clear space equal to the height of the “C” around all marks
  • Use only approved colour combinations: dark, light and red variants
  • Keep to the minimum size — 80px wide digital, 25mm print
  • Use the slab icon and wordmark together, or the wordmark alone
  • Use full-white or full-charcoal monochrome when brand colours are unavailable
✕ Don’t
  • Stretch, skew or distort the logo in any dimension
  • Place the logo on busy photography without a container
  • Recreate the logo in unapproved typefaces — even Syne in the wrong weight
  • Add drop shadows, outlines, glows or decorative effects
  • Place the red variant on non-white or non-light backgrounds

11 · Photography & Imagery

Imagery direction

Authentic over staged, always. CardPop photography should feel like the real hobby — genuine collectors, real cards, honest light.

Graded Slabs

PSA/BGS/CGC slabs on clean surfaces. High-contrast lighting, label detail visible. Avoid overly staged arrangements.

Convention Energy

Card-show booth photography and collector interaction. Busy but organised. Warm ambient lighting.

Tabletop Layouts

Cards fanned on neutral surfaces — dark felt or light wood. Authentic, unposed, natural light.

Collector Portraits

Real collectors, not models. Diverse, caught in the act — sorting, inspecting, trading.

Marketplace UI

Clean device mockups showing the platform in action: listings, search, profiles.

Community Moments

Group shots at shows, trading scenes, celebrating a find. Genuine hobby-culture energy.

✓ Use
  • Authentic hobby photography with real collectors
  • Clean product shots on neutral or charcoal backgrounds
  • Candid convention and trading moments
  • High resolution — min 1200px wide for web
✕ Avoid
  • Generic “business” stock photography
  • Gambling, casino or high-stakes financial imagery
  • Crypto / NFT-adjacent visual clichés
  • Heavily filtered, over-processed images

12 · Motion & Animation

Motion principles

Motion is purposeful and quick. It guides attention, confirms interaction and adds a touch of collector delight — never decoration for its own sake.

Card Reveal

300–400ms · ease-out

Opacity 0→1 + translateY 8px→0. Product card entry and listing reveals.

Hover Elevation

150ms · ease

translateY -2px on card hover. A subtle lift — never a box-shadow; change the border instead.

CTA Press

80ms · ease-in

scale(0.97) on button active. Reinforces the physical interaction feel.

Page Transition

200ms · ease-in-out

Fade opacity between marketplace pages. No slide or bounce on core navigation.

Marketplace Filter

200ms · ease

Grid reflows with an opacity fade. Products fade in/out; layout shifts are instant.

Grade Badge Pop

250ms · spring

scale(0)→scale(1) on badge appear. One moment of collector delight per load.

Accessibility
Brand Style Guide · 2026
13 · Accessibility

Inclusive by default

Accessibility is a baseline, not an add-on. Every collector should be able to browse, buy and sell with ease — on any device, with any ability.

01

Colour contrast

All body text meets WCAG AA (4.5:1). Charcoal on white = 12.6:1. White on CardPop Red = 4.6:1. Never use Silver on white for body text.

02

Typography scale

Minimum 13px for UI text; 16px body base. Scale with user preferences — use rem/em, never lock font sizes in px only.

03

Keyboard navigation

Every interactive element is reachable via Tab. Focus rings: 2px solid CardPop Red with 2px offset. Never remove focus outlines without a replacement.

04

Touch targets

Minimum 44×44px for all interactive elements on mobile. Star ratings must be individually tappable.

05

Alt text

Card images: “[Card name], [Grade], [Year], [Set]”. Seller avatars: “[Name] seller avatar”. Decorative graphics: empty alt.

06

Reduced motion

Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Wrap animation in @media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference); disable card reveals and transitions.

Trust Signals
Brand Style Guide · 2026
14 · Trust & Community

Signals of trust

Trust is the product. Every surface should make it obvious that CardPop is a safe, verified place to trade — because confidence is what turns browsers into collectors.

✓

Seller Verification

ID-checked profiles with a visible green verification badge. Tiers: Verified, PowerSeller, Elite. Shown prominently on every listing card.

✓

Star Ratings

5-star system in CardPop Red with a review count. Minimum 3 reviews before an aggregate shows. Links to the full review page.

✓

Transaction History

Visible sales count on seller profiles — “X sales” badge, positive-feedback percentage, join date and member-tenure badge.

✓

Secure Trade Indicator

Lock icon + “Protected Trade” label on eligible transactions. Escrow and buyer-protection messaging at checkout.
